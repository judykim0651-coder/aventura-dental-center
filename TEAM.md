### Bio
> Dr. David S. Bistritz, a general dentist and the founder of Aventura Dental Center, has been serving the Aventura community since 1989. He developed a passion for healthcare during high school while volunteering at a local hospital dental clinic. Dr. Bistritz specializes in general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, offering treatments such as dental implants, porcelain veneers, and smile makeovers. He earned his dental degree from the University of Maryland Dental School, where he focused on a comprehensive approach to patient care. With over 37 years of experience, he continuously seeks education through conferences and management courses. Under his leadership, Aventura Dental Center has received multiple awards, including the Rapid Registration Award and Speedy Response Award from Zocdoc.
