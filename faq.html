Common Questions

We offer comprehensive dental care for the whole family, including preventive care (checkups and cleanings), cosmetic dentistry (veneers, teeth whitening, and bonding), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures), dental implants, Invisalign clear aligners, root canal therapy, and emergency dental care.

We are located at 18171 Biscayne Blvd, Aventura, FL 33160. We proudly serve patients from Aventura and the surrounding communities, including Hallandale Beach, North Miami Beach, Sunny Isles Beach, and Golden Beach.

Our office is open Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Friday from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

We accept most dental insurance plans and will help you understand your coverage. If you do not have insurance, we offer clear, reasonable pricing and flexible payment options, so give us a call and we will go over what works best for you.

We understand that visiting the dentist can feel stressful. Our team is trained to help anxious patients feel at ease. We explain every step, provide gentle care, and always work at your pace.

Yes. If you are experiencing a dental emergency such as severe tooth pain, a broken tooth, or a lost filling, call us as soon as possible at (305) 933-2501 and we will do our best to see you promptly.

For most patients, we recommend a routine checkup and professional cleaning every six months. Regular visits help us catch potential issues early and keep your smile healthy.

Yes. We offer a full range of cosmetic services, including professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, and complete smile makeovers to help you feel confident about your smile.
