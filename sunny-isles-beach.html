Complete Dental Care Convenient for Sunny Isles Beach Residents

Aventura Dental Center brings general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry together in one place, so Sunny Isles Beach patients rarely need a referral elsewhere. Our preventive services include checkups, cleanings, exams, and x-rays to keep your smile healthy year-round.

When you need more, we are ready. Our restorative treatments include fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, dental implants, All-on-4, and full-mouth reconstruction. For a brighter smile, we offer veneers, teeth whitening, bonding, and complete smile makeovers, along with Invisalign clear aligners.

We also handle root canals, extractions, and emergency dentistry, giving Sunny Isles Beach patients a single trusted practice for nearly every dental need.

Decades of Experience You Can Rely On

Dr. David S. Bistritz, DDS, has cared for smiles in this area since 1989, bringing more than 37 years of experience to every appointment. That depth of experience means Sunny Isles Beach patients benefit from steady, informed care whether they are here for a simple cleaning or a more involved restorative plan.

Our reputation reflects that commitment. With a 4.8-star average across 700-plus Google reviews, patients consistently point to the personal attention and unhurried approach they receive at our Aventura office.
