Preventive Dentistry in Aventura, FL

The healthiest smiles are the ones we protect before problems ever begin. At Aventura Dental Center, Dr. David S. Bistritz and our welcoming team help you stay ahead of tooth decay and gum disease with gentle, thorough preventive care right here in Aventura, FL.

Care That Stops Problems Before They Start

Preventive dentistry is about catching small concerns early, while they are still simple to treat. Regular checkups let us spot cavities, worn fillings, and the first signs of gum disease long before they cause pain or require more involved treatment.

Most preventive visits include a comprehensive exam, a professional cleaning, and digital x-rays when needed. Together these give us a clear picture of your oral health and help us build a care plan tailored to you.

With 37+ years serving Aventura since 1989, Dr. Bistritz is known for honest, gentle care. If your teeth and gums are healthy, we will tell you so and keep you on a routine that maintains it.

What Your Preventive Visit Includes

A routine checkup and cleaning removes the plaque and tartar buildup that everyday brushing and flossing can miss, especially along the gumline and between teeth. This helps prevent cavities and keeps your gums firm and healthy.

Comprehensive exams and digital x-rays let us look beyond the surface to check the health of your teeth, roots, and supporting bone. During these visits we also perform an oral cancer screening, because early detection matters most.

Gum health is a central part of prevention. We monitor for early gum inflammation and, when caught early, help you reverse it before it progresses toward more serious periodontal problems.

Protecting Your Long-Term Oral Health

Consistent preventive care protects more than your smile. Keeping decay and gum disease in check helps you avoid emergencies and preserves your natural teeth for years to come.

Each visit is also a chance to fine-tune your at-home routine. We share practical guidance on brushing, flossing, and habits that fit your daily life, so the care continues between appointments.
