# Google Business Profile Optimization — Aventura Dental Center

**Why this matters:** The "Google AI Mode / near me" gap in the RiseRidge audit is driven almost
entirely by the Google Business Profile (GBP), not the website. GBP is the single highest-ROI local
SEO lever, and it's free. Work through this top-to-bottom. Items are ordered by impact.

**Manage at:** https://business.google.com (or search "Aventura Dental Center" while signed in as the owner)

---

## 0. Access & verification (do first)
- [ ] Confirm the practice **owns/has manager access** to the profile (not the previous developer/agency).
- [ ] Confirm the listing is **verified** (blue check / verified badge).
- [ ] Remove any stale/duplicate listings for the practice (report duplicates).

## 1. Core business info — must EXACTLY match the website (NAP consistency)
- [ ] **Name:** `Aventura Dental Center` (no keyword stuffing like "Aventura Dental Center - Best Dentist")
- [ ] **Address:** `18171 Biscayne Blvd, Aventura, FL 33160`
- [ ] **Phone:** `(305) 933-2501`
- [ ] **Website:** point to the new site once the domain is live (`https://aventuradentalcenter.com`)
- [ ] **Hours:** Mon–Thu 8:00 AM–6:00 PM · Fri 8:00 AM–1:00 PM · Sat–Sun Closed
- [ ] Add **Special Hours** for holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, July 4, etc.)
- [ ] **Appointment/Booking link:** add the PatientsReach URL as the booking action

## 2. Categories (huge for what searches you show up in)
- [ ] **Primary category:** `Dentist`
- [ ] **Add secondary categories** that match services actually offered:
  - Cosmetic Dentist
  - Dental Implants Periodontist (only if applicable) / Dental Implants Provider
  - Emergency Dental Service
  - Teeth Whitening Service
  - Denture Care Center
  - Orthodontist *(only if comfortable — you offer Invisalign, not braces; optional)*
- [ ] Do **not** add categories for services you don't offer (no Pediatric Dentist, no Sedation).

## 3. Services (mirror the website — helps procedure-level visibility)
Add each as a Service with a 1–2 sentence description. Prioritize the high-value ones the audit flagged:
- [ ] Dental Implants · All-on-4 Implants
- [ ] Invisalign / Clear Aligners
- [ ] Veneers · Smile Makeover · Teeth Whitening · Dental Bonding
- [ ] Dental Crowns & Bridges · Dentures · Fillings · Full-Mouth Reconstruction
- [ ] Root Canal Treatment · Tooth Extraction
- [ ] Checkups · Cleanings · Emergency Dentistry
> Tip: use the same wording as the matching website service page so Google connects them.

## 5. Photos & video (add regularly — strong ranking + trust signal)
- [ ] Logo + cover photo
- [ ] **Exterior** (storefront/signage — helps people find you) and **interior** (reception, operatories)
- [ ] Team / Dr. Bistritz photos (use his real photo — see note below)
- [ ] A few treatment / smile before-&-after shots (with patient consent)
- [ ] Add a few new photos **monthly** — Google favors freshly-updated profiles.

## 6. Google Posts (post ~weekly — free reach, feeds AI answers)
Ideas: a service spotlight (implants/Invisalign/veneers), an office update, an oral-health tip, a
new-patient welcome, a review highlight. Each post: 1 image + 1–2 sentences + a "Book" button.
- [ ] Set a recurring weekly reminder to post.

## 7. Q&A (seed it — owner can post questions AND answers)
Pre-load the common ones so the answers are controlled:
- [ ] "Do you accept new patients?" · "What are your hours?" · "Do you offer emergency dentistry?"
- [ ] "Do you offer Invisalign / implants / veneers?" · "Where do you park / how do I find you?"
- [ ] "Do you see patients without insurance?" (answer honestly, general)

## 8. Reviews (biggest ongoing lever — tied to ranking AND the AI recommendations)
- [ ] **Respond to every review** — positive and negative — professionally. (You have 700+; keep the response habit going.)
- [ ] Set up a **review-request flow**: text/email patients your Google review short-link after visits.
  - Get the short link from the GBP "Ask for reviews" button (looks like `g.page/r/...`).
- [ ] Target a **steady drip** of new reviews (a few per week) rather than bursts.

## 9. Attributes & extras
- [ ] Accessibility (wheelchair-accessible entrance/restroom/parking, as applicable)
- [ ] "Appointments recommended," "Accepts new patients," languages spoken (English/Spanish if true)
- [ ] Enable **Messaging** only if the front desk can respond quickly.

## 10. Citations & consistency (supporting off-GBP)
- [ ] Ensure NAP is **identical** on Yelp, Facebook, Instagram, Healthgrades, Vitals, and local directories.
- [ ] Chamber of Commerce / local business directory listings (a few quality ones — not mass submissions).

---

### The 20% that drives 80% of the result
1. Correct **categories** + full **services** list.
2. **Respond to all reviews** + a steady **new-review** drip.
3. **Weekly Google Posts** + fresh **photos** monthly.
4. **NAP identical** everywhere, website pointed at the new site.

Do those four consistently and the "Google AI Mode / near me" gap closes on its own — no agency retainer required.
