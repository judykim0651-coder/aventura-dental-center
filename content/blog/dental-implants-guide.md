## Trends in Implant Dentistry

**Content Wireframe:**

### AI in Implant Planning
- AI-driven software analyzes CBCT scans to suggest optimal implant size, angle, and depth.
- Reduces human error, speeds up surgical planning.

### Digital Workflows
- Intraoral scanners (ITERO) eliminate messy impressions.
- Same-day milling of crowns via CEREC.
- 3D-printed surgical guides for flapless surgery.

### New Materials
- Zirconia implants as a metal-free alternative to titanium.
- Improved osseointegration surfaces (hydrophilic, nanostructured).
- **Visual suggestion:** Infographic — "The Digital Implant Workflow: Scan → Plan → Guide → Place → Restore."

## Recovery, Maintenance, and Success

**Content Wireframe:**

### Immediate Post-Op (Days 1–7)
- Soft food diet, ice packs, prescribed mouth rinse.
- What's normal: swelling, mild discomfort, minor bleeding.

### Long-Term Maintenance
- Daily brushing + flossing (including superfloss or water flosser around implant).
- Professional cleanings every 6 months — hygienist uses specialized implant scalers.
- Regular X-rays to check bone levels.

### Success Factors
- Smoking cessation (smokers have 2× higher failure rate).
- Nightguard if bruxism is present.
- Annual implant recall appointments.
