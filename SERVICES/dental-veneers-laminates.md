## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## What Are Dental Veneers and Laminates?                                │
│                                                                             │
│  Dental veneers (also called laminates) are ultra-thin, custom-made shells  │
│  that are bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They are designed to   │
│  correct a range of cosmetic concerns — including chips, cracks, stains,    │
│  gaps, unevenness, and slight misalignment — in as few as two visits.       │
│                                                                             │
│  Veneers are typically made from porcelain or composite resin. Porcelain    │
│  veneers are especially popular for their lifelike translucency, stain      │
│  resistance, and durability. Each veneer is meticulously crafted to match   │
│  the shape, size, and color that complements your natural smile and facial  │
│  features.                                                                  │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care for Your Veneers                                           │
│                                                                             │
│  • **Brush** at least twice daily with a non-abrasive toothpaste (avoid      │
│    whitening or charcoal toothpastes)                                       │
│  • **Floss** daily to keep the gum line around veneers healthy              │
│  • **Avoid** biting hard objects (fingernails, pens, ice) that could chip   │
│    the veneer                                                               │
│  • **Limit** stain-heavy foods — though porcelain resists stains, the       │
│    bonding edge can still absorb color over time                             │
│  • **Wear** a nightguard if you grind or clench your teeth                  │
│  • **Visit** your dentist for regular checkups to monitor veneer condition  │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
