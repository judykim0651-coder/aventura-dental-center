## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## Invisalign vs Traditional Braces — Which Is Right for You?             │
│                                                                             │
│  Both Invisalign clear aligners and traditional braces are effective ways   │
│  to straighten teeth and correct bite issues, but they work differently.    │
│  Traditional braces use metal or ceramic brackets bonded to each tooth,     │
│  connected by an archwire that applies continuous pressure to gradually     │
│  move teeth into correct alignment. Invisalign uses a series of custom,    │
│  removable clear plastic aligner trays that are replaced every 1–2 weeks   │
│  to shift teeth incrementally.                                              │
│                                                                             │
│  The best option depends on your specific orthodontic needs, lifestyle,     │
│  and preferences. Braces are typically more effective for complex           │
│  movements, severe crowding, and bite corrections. Invisalign offers a      │
│  nearly invisible appearance, easier oral hygiene, and fewer dietary        │
│  restrictions. During your consultation, we'll evaluate your case and       │
│  help you choose the approach that aligns with your goals.                  │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

│                                                                             │
│  ### Invisalign                                                              │
│  Step 1 ────►  Digital scan and 3D treatment plan showing your projected    │

│  Step 2 ────►  Custom aligners fabricated — you receive a full series        │

│  Step 3 ────►  Wear aligners 20–22 hours/day — remove only for eating and   │
│               brushing                                                       │
│  Step 4 ────►  Switch to new trays every 1–2 weeks                          │
│  Step 5 ────►  Check-in visits every 6–8 weeks to track progress            │
│  Step 6 ────►  Retainer phase after active treatment to maintain results    │
│                                                                             │
│  ### Traditional Braces                                                      │
│  Step 1 ────►  Brackets bonded to teeth and archwire inserted               │
│  Step 2 ────►  Regular adjustments every 4–8 weeks to tighten wires         │
│  Step 3 ────►  Elastics (rubber bands) added if needed for bite correction  │
│  Step 4 ────►  Average treatment time: 18–24 months                         │
│  Step 5 ────►  Braces removed and retainers fitted to hold results          │

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care During Orthodontic Treatment                                │
│                                                                             │
│  ### With Invisalign                                                         │
│  • **Clean** aligners daily with a soft brush and clear antibacterial soap  │
│  • **Rinse** aligners every time you remove them to avoid bacteria buildup  │
│  • **Brush** and floss after every meal before reinserting trays            │
│  • **Store** aligners in their case when not in your mouth                  │
│  • **Avoid** hot water — it can warp the plastic                           │
│                                                                             │
│  ### With Braces                                                             │
│  • **Brush** after every meal using an orthodontic toothbrush               │
│  • **Floss** daily with a floss threader or water flosser                   │
│  • **Avoid** sticky, hard, or chewy foods that can damage brackets          │
│  • **Use** wax on brackets that cause irritation                            │
│  • **Attend** all adjustment appointments to stay on track                  │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
