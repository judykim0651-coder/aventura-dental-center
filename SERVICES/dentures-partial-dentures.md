## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## What Are Dentures and Partial Dentures?                                │
│                                                                             │
│  Dentures are custom-made removable appliances that replace missing teeth   │
│  and surrounding tissue. Full dentures replace an entire arch (upper or     │
│  lower) when all teeth are missing. Partial dentures fill in gaps when      │
│  some natural teeth remain, attaching to adjacent teeth with precision      │
│  clasps or connectors for a secure fit.                                     │
│                                                                             │
│  Modern dentures are crafted from high-quality acrylic and sometimes        │
│  reinforced with metal or flexible materials for added durability and       │
│  comfort. They restore your ability to chew a wider variety of foods,      │
│  speak clearly, and smile with confidence — improving both function and     │
│  quality of life.                                                           │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care for Your Dentures                                          │
│                                                                             │
│  • **Remove and rinse** dentures after eating to remove food debris         │
│  • **Brush** dentures daily with a soft denture brush and non-abrasive       │
│    denture cleaner — not regular toothpaste                                 │
│  • **Soak** overnight in denture solution or water to keep them moist       │
│    and prevent warping                                                      │
│  • **Handle** with care — hold over a towel or water basin if dropped       │
│  • **Clean** your gums, tongue, and any remaining teeth daily               │
│  • **Visit** your dentist for annual checkups to evaluate fit and           │
│    reline if needed                                                         │
│  • **Replace** dentures every 5–7 years or as your dentist recommends       │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
