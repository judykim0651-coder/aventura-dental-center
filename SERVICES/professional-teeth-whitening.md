## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## What Is Professional Teeth Whitening?                                  │
│                                                                             │
│  Professional teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental treatment that uses      │
│  high-concentration bleaching agents — applied under controlled conditions  │
│  — to lighten the shade of your teeth. Unlike over-the-counter strips or   │
│  trays, professional whitening is supervised by a dentist who ensures the   │
│  treatment is safe for your enamel and gums. The active ingredient,         │
│  typically hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, penetrates the enamel   │
│  to break down stain compounds from coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco, and    │
│  aging.                                                                     │
│                                                                             │
│  In-office whitening can brighten your smile by several shades in as little │
│  as one hour. We also offer professional take-home whitening kits with      │
│  custom-fitted trays that provide gradual, convenient whitening over        │
│  1–2 weeks. Both options deliver superior results compared to drugstore     │
│  products — with less risk of gum irritation or uneven whitening.           │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care After Teeth Whitening                                      │
│                                                                             │
│  • **Wait** at least 48 hours before consuming staining foods and drinks     │
│    (coffee, tea, red wine, berries, soy sauce, curry)                       │
│  • **Avoid** smoking or tobacco for at least 48 hours — teeth are more      │
│    porous and absorb stains easily right after treatment                    │
│  • **Brush** gently with a soft-bristle toothbrush and non-abrasive         │
│    toothpaste                                                               │
│  • **Rinse** with water after eating or drinking anything that could stain  │
│  • **Use** a straw for dark beverages to minimize contact with front teeth  │
│  • **Schedule** touch-up sessions as recommended to maintain your results   │
│  • **Practice** good oral hygiene — brushing and flossing help extend       │
│    whitening longevity                                                       │
│  • **Consider** a whitening toothpaste for maintenance (use 2–3 times/week) │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
