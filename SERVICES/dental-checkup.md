## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## What Is a Dental Checkup?                                              │
│                                                                             │
│  A dental checkup is a routine preventive visit that combines a thorough    │
│  examination of your teeth, gums, and mouth with a professional cleaning.   │
│  These appointments are the foundation of good oral health, allowing your   │
│  dentist to detect issues like cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer in    │
│  their earliest — and most treatable — stages.                              │
│                                                                             │
│  The American Dental Association recommends visiting your dentist every     │
│  six months for a regular checkup. Consistent care helps prevent small      │
│  problems from turning into costly, complex procedures down the road.       │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care for Your Teeth Between Checkups                            │
│                                                                             │
│  • **Brush** twice a day for two full minutes with fluoride toothpaste      │
│  • **Floss** daily to clean between teeth where your brush can't reach      │
│  • **Limit** sugary snacks and acidic drinks that erode enamel              │
│  • **Drink** plenty of water — especially after meals                       │
│  • **Replace** your toothbrush every 3–4 months or sooner if bristles fray  │
│  • **Schedule** your next checkup before leaving the office                 │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
