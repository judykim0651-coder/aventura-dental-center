All-on-4 Implants

All-on-4 Implants

Replace a full arch of missing teeth with a stable, natural-looking implant solution that restores confidence and everyday function.

Book an Appointment

What Are All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses four strategically placed dental implants to support a complete set of fixed replacement teeth. Unlike individual implants, the All-on-4 concept replaces an entire upper or lower arch with fewer implants, reducing the need for bone grafting and shortening treatment time.

This procedure is ideal for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth and want a stable, permanent alternative to dentures. The implants act as artificial tooth roots, preserving jawbone structure while providing a secure foundation for a natural-looking prosthetic arch.

Benefits of All-on-4 Implants

No Slipping

Fixed in place — no clicking or movement like traditional dentures.

Natural Looking

Custom-matched prosthetic blends with your smile and facial structure.

Fast Recovery

Often same-day option available with reduced treatment timeline.

Strong Chewing

Chewing force similar to natural teeth.

  • Minimal bone grafting needed — posterior implants placed at an angle
  • Faster treatment timeline compared to individual implants per arch
  • Fixed restoration — not removable like traditional dentures
  • Preserves facial structure — prevents sunken appearance from bone loss

What to Expect — All-on-4 Procedure

  1. Comprehensive exam with 3D imaging to evaluate bone density and plan implant placement.
  2. Implant placement surgery — four implants positioned in the jaw (two straight, two angled).
  3. Temporary fixed arch attached while implants integrate with bone (osseointegration) — typically 4–6 months.
  4. Final custom permanent arch delivered and secured to the implants.
  5. Follow-up visits to monitor healing and ensure proper fit.

How to Care for Your All-on-4 Implants

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle brush, paying attention to the gum line and around the abutments.
  • Floss or use a water flosser to clean under and around the fixed arch.
  • Rinse with an antimicrobial mouthwash to reduce bacteria.
  • Avoid chewing hard objects (ice, pens) that could damage the arch.
  • Visit your dentist regularly for professional cleanings and checkups.

Key Features of All-on-4 Implants

  • Full arch restoration on just 4 implants
  • Tilted posterior implants maximize existing bone
  • Same-day temporary teeth option in many cases
  • Digital planning for precise placement
  • Custom acrylic or porcelain fixed arch
  • Compatible with upper and lower arches
  • High success rate with proper care
  • Covered by many dental insurance plans (partial)

Is All-on-4 Right for You?

  • Multiple missing or failing teeth — All-on-4 is designed for patients who have lost most or all of the teeth in an arch, or whose remaining teeth are no longer restorable.
  • Denture wearers seeking a fixed solution — patients who find traditional dentures uncomfortable or unstable often benefit from the security of a fixed implant-supported arch.
  • Adequate or graftable bone — the angled posterior implants are designed to use available bone in areas where density is often better preserved, reducing the need for grafting in many cases; Dr. Bistritz will assess your bone levels with 3D imaging.
  • Ready to commit to maintenance — long-term success depends on consistent home care and routine professional cleanings and checkups.

The only way to confirm candidacy is a consultation with Dr. Bistritz. He will review your oral health, imaging, and goals to outline the right treatment path for you.

What to Expect on Treatment Day and Beyond

  1. Planning and 3D imaging — detailed scans allow Dr. Bistritz to map exact implant positions before any procedure begins, improving precision and predictability.
  2. Tooth extraction (if needed) — any remaining teeth in the arch that cannot be saved are removed, typically at the same appointment as implant placement.
  3. Placement of four implants — two implants are positioned upright in the front of the jaw and two are angled toward the back, maximizing contact with available bone.
  4. Temporary fixed arch — in many cases a provisional arch is attached the same day, so patients leave with a functional set of fixed teeth while the implants heal.
  5. Osseointegration healing window — the bone fuses to the implants over a healing period that typically spans several months; follow-up visits monitor progress throughout.
  6. Final custom restoration — once healing is confirmed, the permanent arch is fabricated and secured to the implants, completing the full-arch restoration.

Timelines and same-day options depend on individual anatomy and healing. Dr. Bistritz will give you a personalized schedule at your consultation.

Living With Your All-on-4 Restoration

Once your final arch is in place, most patients find they can enjoy a full range of everyday activities with confidence. The fixed restoration does not shift or require adhesives, and chewing function is substantially improved compared to traditional dentures.

  • Eating — follow your dentist's soft-food guidance during the healing phase; after the final restoration is placed, your care team will advise on foods that support long-term durability.
  • Cleaning — brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush, paying careful attention to the gum line and around each abutment. A water flosser or implant-specific floss helps reach under the arch where a standard toothbrush cannot.
  • Routine checkups — regular professional cleanings and examinations allow Dr. Bistritz to monitor the health of the implants, gum tissue, and the fit of the arch over time.

All-on-4 Implant Questions

Traditional dental implants replace individual teeth, each requiring its own implant post. All-on-4 uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of replacement teeth. The angled positioning of the posterior implants often allows this to be done without the bone grafting that individual implants sometimes require, making it a more streamlined option for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in an arch.

In many cases a temporary fixed arch is placed on the same day as implant surgery, so patients often leave with functional teeth that day. This temporary restoration stays in place during the osseointegration healing period, after which the final custom arch is attached. Whether same-day placement is appropriate for you depends on your individual bone structure and healing assessment, which Dr. Bistritz will review at your consultation.

During the initial healing period you will be asked to follow a soft-food diet to protect the implants while the bone fuses. Once healing is complete and the final arch is in place, most patients can enjoy a wide variety of foods. Your dentist will provide specific dietary guidance based on your progress at each follow-up visit.

A fixed All-on-4 arch is cleaned in place rather than removed. Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle brush, paying close attention to the gum line and around each abutment. A water flosser or implant-specific floss is recommended to clean under the arch where a regular toothbrush cannot reach. Routine professional cleanings allow Dr. Bistritz to check the fit and health of the restoration.

Many long-term denture wearers are good candidates for All-on-4, even if they have experienced some bone loss over time. The angled implant design is specifically intended to work with the bone that remains in the back of the jaw. Dr. Bistritz will evaluate your bone levels with 3D imaging at a consultation to determine the best approach for your situation.
