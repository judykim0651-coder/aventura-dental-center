What Are Dental Cleaning and Examinations?

Regular dental cleanings and examinations are the foundation of good oral health. A professional cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains that daily brushing and flossing cannot reach. Combined with a thorough exam, this routine visit helps catch issues like cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer in their earliest stages — before they become bigger, more expensive problems.

We recommend scheduling a cleaning and exam every six months. During your visit, our hygienist will perform a periodontal evaluation and prophylaxis cleaning, and the dentist will conduct a comprehensive check of your teeth, gums, and oral tissues. These regular visits are the most effective way to maintain a healthy smile and prevent the need for more extensive dental work down the road.

What to Expect — Dental Cleaning and Exam Visit

  1. Medical history review and any updates to your health conditions or medications.
  2. Digital X-rays (if due) to check for decay between teeth and beneath existing restorations.
  3. Periodontal exam — gum pocket measurements to evaluate gum health and screen for periodontal disease.
  4. Professional cleaning — scaling to remove tartar, followed by polishing and flossing.
  5. Oral cancer screening — visual and tactile exam of soft tissues, tongue, and throat.
  6. Dentist's exam — final check and personalized recommendations for your next steps.

How to Care Between Cleanings

  • Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to clean between teeth and below the gum line.
  • Rinse with an alcohol-free mouthwash to reduce bacteria.
  • Limit sugary snacks and acidic beverages that erode enamel.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months or sooner if bristles fray.
  • Stay on schedule — book your next 6-month cleaning before leaving.

What a Professional Cleaning Includes

A professional dental cleaning reaches areas that daily brushing and flossing typically miss. Using specialized instruments, your hygienist clears away the buildup that collects along the gum line and between teeth, then polishes each tooth so your smile feels smooth and looks its brightest. Here is what a cleaning at our Aventura, FL office generally involves.

  • Removal of plaque and tartar (scaling) — hardened buildup along the gum line and between the teeth is gently removed with hand instruments or an ultrasonic scaler.
  • Professional polishing — a lightly gritty paste smooths the tooth surface and lifts many everyday stains for a cleaner, brighter finish.
  • Checking gum health — your hygienist looks for redness, swelling, or bleeding and may measure the pockets around each tooth to screen for early gum disease.
  • A review of your brushing and flossing technique — practical, personalized tips for the spots that tend to collect buildup between visits.

Why Regular Cleanings Matter

Even with excellent daily care, plaque hardens into tartar that only a professional cleaning can remove. Keeping a consistent schedule — for most patients, about twice a year — protects your teeth and gums and gives Dr. Bistritz the chance to spot small issues early, while they are simpler to treat. If you want to stay ahead of problems, our broader preventive dentistry care builds on these routine visits.

  • Prevent cavities and gum disease — removing plaque and tartar reduces the bacteria that lead to decay and gum inflammation.
  • Fresher breath — clearing away buildup and bacteria helps keep your breath fresh between visits.
  • Early detection — routine exams can catch cavities, gum problems, and other concerns before they become painful or costly.
  • A brighter smile — polishing lifts many surface stains left by coffee, tea, and other everyday foods.

What to Expect, Step by Step

  1. Exam and any needed X-rays — Dr. Bistritz reviews your teeth and gums, and digital X-rays may be taken if they are due to check areas the eye cannot see.
  2. Scaling to remove buildup — plaque and tartar are carefully cleared from the tooth surfaces and along the gum line.
  3. Polishing — each tooth is polished to smooth the surface and lift many everyday stains.
  4. Flossing and fluoride if recommended — your hygienist flosses between the teeth and may apply a fluoride treatment to help strengthen enamel.
  5. Personalized home-care tips — you leave with specific guidance for your smile and, if helpful, a reminder to book your next visit.

Caring for Your Smile Between Cleanings

What you do at home between appointments has the biggest impact on your long-term oral health. A few consistent habits keep plaque under control and make each professional cleaning easier and more effective. Pairing good home care with a routine dental checkup is the most reliable way to keep your smile healthy.

  • Brush gently twice a day for two minutes with a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between your teeth daily with floss or another interdental cleaner to reach where a brush cannot.
  • Rinse and hydrate — an alcohol-free mouthwash and plenty of water help wash away food and bacteria.
  • Be mindful of sugar and acid — limiting sugary and acidic snacks and drinks helps protect your enamel.
  • Keep your schedule — book your next cleaning so buildup never gets the chance to accumulate.

Cleaning & Exam Questions

For most patients, a professional cleaning and exam about every six months helps keep teeth and gums healthy. If you are prone to gum disease or have other risk factors, Dr. Bistritz may suggest more frequent visits. He will recommend the schedule that best fits your oral health.

A routine cleaning is typically comfortable, and most patients feel only mild pressure or vibration as plaque and tartar are removed. If your gums are sensitive or inflamed, let your hygienist know — steps can be taken to keep you comfortable throughout the visit.

A routine cleaning and exam generally takes about 45 minutes to an hour, though the exact time depends on how much buildup is present and whether X-rays are due. If more extensive care is needed, Dr. Bistritz will discuss your options before moving ahead.
