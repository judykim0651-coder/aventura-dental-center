## Detailed Description

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## What Are Dental Cleaning and Examinations?                              │
│                                                                             │
│  Regular dental cleanings and examinations are the foundation of good oral  │
│  health. A professional cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains  │
│  that daily brushing and flossing cannot reach. Combined with a thorough    │
│  exam, this routine visit helps catch issues like cavities, gum disease,    │
│  and oral cancer in their earliest stages — before they become bigger,      │
│  more expensive problems.                                                   │
│                                                                             │
│  We recommend scheduling a cleaning and exam every six months. During your  │
│  visit, our hygienist will perform a periodontal evaluation and            │
│  prophylaxis cleaning, and the dentist will conduct a comprehensive check   │
│  of your teeth, gums, and oral tissues. These regular visits are the most   │
│  effective way to maintain a healthy smile and prevent the need for more    │
│  extensive dental work down the road.                                       │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## How to Care

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                             │
│  ## How to Care Between Cleanings                                           │
│                                                                             │
│  • **Brush** twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste            │
│  • **Floss** daily to clean between teeth and below the gum line             │
│  • **Rinse** with an alcohol-free mouthwash to reduce bacteria               │
│  • **Limit** sugary snacks and acidic beverages that erode enamel            │
│  • **Replace** your toothbrush every 3–4 months or sooner if bristles fray  │
│  • **Stay on schedule** — book your next 6-month cleaning before leaving     │
│  • **Watch** for warning signs: bleeding gums, sensitivity, or persistent    │
│    bad breath                                                                │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
