What Is a Dental Checkup?

A dental checkup is a routine preventive visit that combines a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and mouth with a professional cleaning. These appointments are the foundation of good oral health, allowing your dentist to detect issues like cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer in their earliest — and most treatable — stages.

The American Dental Association recommends visiting your dentist every six months for a regular checkup. Consistent care helps prevent small problems from turning into costly, complex procedures down the road.

What to Expect During a Dental Checkup

  1. Review of medical history and any concerns you may have.
  2. Digital or traditional X-rays to check between teeth and below the gum line.
  3. Comprehensive exam — teeth, gums, tongue, soft tissues, and existing restorations.
  4. Professional dental cleaning — scaling, polishing, and fluoride treatment.
  5. Discussion of findings and personalized care recommendations.

How to Care for Your Teeth Between Checkups

  • Brush twice a day for two full minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to clean between teeth where your brush can't reach.
  • Limit sugary snacks and acidic drinks that erode enamel.
  • Drink plenty of water — especially after meals.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months or sooner if bristles fray.
  • Schedule your next checkup before leaving the office.

What's Included in a Comprehensive Dental Checkup

A comprehensive dental checkup is about far more than a quick look at your teeth. During your visit to our Aventura, FL office, Dr. David S. Bistritz reviews the health of your entire mouth so that nothing is overlooked and small concerns are caught while they are still easy to treat.

  • Thorough exam of your teeth and restorations — checking for decay, wear, and the condition of any existing fillings, crowns, or other dental work.
  • Digital X-rays when needed — used to reveal problems between teeth and below the gum line that are not visible during a visual exam.
  • Oral cancer screening — a careful check of your lips, tongue, cheeks, and soft tissues for any early warning signs.
  • Gum and periodontal evaluation — assessing the health of your gums to spot the earliest signs of gum disease.
  • Bite check — reviewing how your teeth come together to identify grinding, uneven wear, or alignment concerns.
  • Review of your dental history — discussing past treatment, current symptoms, and any changes since your last visit.

How Often Should You Have a Dental Checkup?

For most patients, Dr. Bistritz recommends a routine dental checkup about every six months. This twice-a-year rhythm works well for the majority of healthy adults and children, giving your dentist regular opportunities to monitor your teeth and gums and to remove the plaque and tartar that build up between visits.

Some patients benefit from seeing the dentist more often. If you have a history of gum disease, a higher risk of cavities, or certain health conditions, Dr. Bistritz may recommend more frequent checkups so your oral health can be watched closely. He will advise the schedule that best fits your needs.

Routine visits matter because many dental problems develop quietly. Cavities, gum disease, and other issues often cause no pain in their early stages, so they can go unnoticed until they become more serious. Seeing your dentist on a regular schedule means these concerns are frequently caught early — when treatment is typically simpler, more comfortable, and less involved. To see how checkups fit into a complete prevention plan, explore our approach to preventive dentistry.

What to Expect During Your Checkup, Step by Step

  1. Warm welcome and health review — we begin by updating your medical and dental history and talking through any symptoms or goals you have.
  2. Examination of your teeth and gums — Dr. Bistritz examines each tooth, your existing restorations, and the health of your gums.
  3. Digital X-rays if needed — images may be taken to check areas that cannot be seen during the visual exam.
  4. Oral cancer screening — your lips, tongue, cheeks, and soft tissues are checked for anything unusual.
  5. Professional cleaning — plaque and tartar are removed and your teeth are polished, much like a routine dental cleaning and examination.
  6. Review and next steps — Dr. Bistritz explains what he found, answers your questions, and recommends any care or follow-up.

Every visit is tailored to you. If Dr. Bistritz notices something that needs attention, he will walk you through your options before any treatment is scheduled.

Keeping Your Smile Healthy Between Visits

The care you give your teeth at home between appointments has a lasting effect on your oral health. A consistent daily routine keeps plaque under control and helps every checkup go smoothly. A few simple habits go a long way:

  • Brush twice a day for two full minutes with a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to clean between teeth where a brush cannot reach.
  • Eat a balanced diet and limit sugary or acidic foods and drinks that can wear down enamel.
  • Stay hydrated with water throughout the day to help rinse away food and bacteria.
  • Avoid tobacco, which raises your risk of gum disease and other oral health problems.
  • Ask about whitening — if you would like to brighten your smile, our professional teeth whitening is a safe option to discuss at your visit.
