What Is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover is a customized treatment plan that combines one or more cosmetic and restorative dental procedures to transform your smile. Whether you are dealing with stained, chipped, gapped, crooked, or missing teeth, a smile makeover addresses your unique concerns through a coordinated combination of treatments.

Every makeover begins with a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your goals, assess your oral health, and design a step-by-step plan. Common procedures include teeth whitening, veneers, crowns, bonding, Invisalign, and implants — all working together to create a smile that looks natural, balanced, and beautiful.

What to Expect — Smile Makeover Process

  1. Smile consultation — discuss your goals, concerns, and expectations with your dentist.
  2. Comprehensive exam — X-rays, photos, impressions, and gum health evaluation.
  3. Custom treatment plan designed — digital smile preview and procedure timeline.
  4. Sequential treatment — each procedure performed in the planned order (e.g., whitening, then bonding, then veneers).
  5. Final result and follow-up — adjustments as needed, then maintenance schedule.

How to Care for Your New Smile

  • Brush twice daily with a non-abrasive fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss daily to protect restorations and maintain gum health
  • Avoid stain-causing foods and drinks (coffee, red wine, tobacco)
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind or clench your teeth
  • Visit your dentist every six months for checkups and cleanings
  • Return for periodic touch-ups (whitening, bonding polish) as needed

Is a Smile Makeover Right for You?

A smile makeover is a good fit for adults who have multiple cosmetic or aesthetic concerns and want to address them through a single, coordinated plan rather than one-off treatments over time. Dr. Bistritz evaluates each patient individually at a consultation to determine whether a makeover approach is appropriate.

  • Stained or discolored teeth — deep discoloration that does not respond fully to at-home whitening may benefit from professional whitening, veneers, or bonding as part of a combined plan.
  • Chipped, cracked, or worn teeth — minor chips and surface wear can be restored with bonding; more extensive damage may call for veneers or crowns to rebuild the tooth properly.
  • Gaps or mild spacing issues — gaps between teeth can be closed with bonding, veneers, or Invisalign clear aligners depending on the cause and extent.
  • Uneven or misaligned teeth — when alignment affects both the look and function of the smile, Invisalign may be incorporated as a first stage before cosmetic finishing work.
  • Healthy teeth and gums as a foundation — active decay or gum disease is addressed first; once your oral health is stable, cosmetic work can proceed on a solid foundation.
  • A desire for a coordinated result — patients who want all concerns planned and sequenced together — rather than treated in isolation — are ideal makeover candidates.

The best way to confirm whether a smile makeover is the right path is a personal consultation with Dr. Bistritz, where your oral health, goals, and realistic options are reviewed together.

How We Plan Your Smile Makeover

  1. Consultation and goal-setting — Dr. Bistritz listens to your concerns, reviews your dental history, and discusses what you want to achieve. There is no pressure to commit to any procedure at this stage.
  2. Comprehensive exam and smile evaluation — X-rays, photos, and a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and bite establish a health baseline and reveal what each concern requires clinically.
  3. Coordinated treatment plan — Dr. Bistritz designs a step-by-step plan that may combine the practice's real services as needed: professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, Invisalign clear aligners, and crowns or bridges. Every plan is specific to you — no two makeovers are identical.
  4. Staged treatment in sequence — procedures are carried out in a planned order. Alignment or foundational work (such as Invisalign or crowns) typically precedes surface cosmetics (such as veneers or whitening) so that final results are stable and cohesive.
  5. Final result review — once all stages are complete, Dr. Bistritz reviews the outcome with you, confirms fit and comfort, and outlines a maintenance schedule to protect your investment.

Your specific timeline will be outlined at your consultation. Duration depends on which procedures are included and how many visits each requires.

What to Expect During and After Your Makeover

Because a smile makeover is a staged process, most patients see visible progress at each appointment rather than waiting until the very end. The experience varies depending on which procedures are part of your plan, but the following general points apply broadly.

  • Multiple visits over a planned schedule — each procedure requires its own appointment or series of appointments. Dr. Bistritz sequences them so that healing from one stage does not delay the next.
  • Progress you can see along the way — early stages such as whitening or Invisalign aligner changes produce visible changes before the final procedures begin, which many patients find motivating.
  • Post-procedure sensitivity is normal — some treatments (whitening, veneer preparation) may cause temporary tooth sensitivity. Dr. Bistritz will guide you on what to expect and how to manage it between visits.
  • Aftercare specific to each treatment — veneers require non-abrasive toothpaste and avoiding biting hard objects; bonding benefits from limiting stain-causing foods in the early weeks; Invisalign requires consistent wear and retainer use after treatment ends.
  • Routine checkups protect long-term results — professional cleanings and periodic exams allow Dr. Bistritz to monitor restorations, catch any early wear, and keep your gums healthy so the results last.

Smile Makeover Questions

A smile makeover is a customized treatment plan in which Dr. Bistritz combines one or more cosmetic and restorative procedures — such as professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, Invisalign clear aligners, and crowns or bridges — to address multiple concerns in a coordinated sequence. The goal is a balanced, natural-looking result tailored to your face, goals, and oral health.

At Aventura Dental Center, a smile makeover may draw from the practice's real cosmetic and restorative services: professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, Invisalign clear aligners for alignment, and dental crowns or bridges for structural concerns. Dr. Bistritz selects only the procedures that are clinically appropriate and aligned with your goals — not every makeover includes every treatment.

The duration depends on which procedures are included and how many treatment visits are needed. A plan centered on whitening and bonding may be completed in a few appointments, while a makeover that includes Invisalign or multiple veneers typically spans several months. Dr. Bistritz will outline a realistic timeline at your consultation based on your specific plan.

Dr. Bistritz designs each makeover with facial harmony in mind — considering your tooth proportions, gum line, lip position, and skin tone. Restorations such as veneers and crowns are custom-crafted and shade-matched so they complement your natural teeth rather than stand out. The aim is a smile that looks like a naturally great version of yours.
